National Air Quality Standard (NAAQS) & CPCB Categorization

1. National Air Quality Index (AQI) Categories

AQI Band Category Color Code Health Impact
0 - 50Good#10b981Minimal impact on respiratory health.
51 - 100Satisfactory#84cc16Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive individuals.
101 - 200Moderate#f59e0bBreathing discomfort to people with asthma and heart disease.
201 - 300Poor#f97316Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure.
301 - 400Very Poor#ef4444Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure.
401 - 500Severe#7c3aedAffects healthy people and severely impacts those with existing conditions.

2. Primary Monitored Pollutants

  • PM2.5 & PM10: Fine particulate matter measured in µg/m³.
  • Nitrogen Dioxide (NO&sub2;): Vehicular and thermal emission tracer.
  • Sulphur Dioxide (SO&sub2;): Industrial and fossil combustive index.
  • Carbon Monoxide (CO): High-density urban traffic indicator.
